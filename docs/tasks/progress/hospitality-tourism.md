# Hospitality & Tourism Build Checkpoint

**Mode:** Build, from `docs/tasks/specs/hospitality-tourism.md`.
**Career:** Hospitality & Tourism (`hospitality-tourism`)
**Career status:** `available` (`review_required: false`); the team should still read the outline and content.
**Audience:** Senior high school TVL and humanities learners, college students, and first-year HM/TM students.
**Scope:** Build both tracks and all 12 courses in this run.
**Estimated duration:** Each four-month estimate is an app study-time label only, not a promise or training requirement.

## Content constraints

- Include “educational orientation, not operating or certification training” in Lesson 1 of every course.
- Include DOT, CHED, and TESDA at name level only in the last course of each track, with a direction to official sources for current information.
- Include a closing roles-and-continued-learning lesson in Lesson 10 of each last course.
- Keep service attitude, cultural sensitivity, and respect for guests, coworkers, and local communities recurring.
- Describe shift work, weekends, holidays, and busy seasons honestly without glamorizing or discouraging hospitality careers.
- Treat Philippine regions, Indigenous communities, and heritage sites respectfully. Avoid stereotypes and claims about named communities’ beliefs, rituals, or practices.
- Keep Hospitality Operations focused on hotels, food service, events, and hospitality business; Tourism and Travel on tourism contexts, destinations, tours, travel services, and responsible tourism.
- Do not include food-safety temperatures, times, procedures, certificate claims, allergy or medical advice, alcohol-service rules or legal age limits, statistics, tourist figures, salaries, hiring or overseas-demand claims, prices, rates, or revenue figures.
- Do not include visa, immigration, travel-advisory, entry requirement, or destination-specific safety/health details.
- Do not name or rank brands, hotels, restaurants, airlines, tour operators, or booking platforms. Do not include agency order numbers, accreditation details, exam/assessment dates, fees, or passing rates.
- Do not state specific facts, dates, or figures about named destinations or heritage sites, or make claims about named Indigenous communities’ beliefs, rituals, or practices.
- Use no images, diagrams, or links. Use fictional, general examples and only `quickCheck`, `order`, and `matchPairs` activities.
- Keep lesson titles and objectives distinct across tracks.

## Track 1: Hospitality Operations

4. Restaurant and Food-Service Settings — Compare food-service settings by guest occasion and service context.
5. Events and Banquet Venues — Explain how event settings bring people and service teams together.
6. Guest Needs and Expectations — Describe how guest needs shape welcoming service.

10. Hospitality Roles and Continued Learning — Summarize sector roles and identify ways to continue learning.
