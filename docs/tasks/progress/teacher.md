Subtitle: Teach young learners in Kindergarten and Grades 1 to 6.

6. Classroom observation and reflection — Objective: explain why teachers observe and reflect on practice.
7. Collaboration with families and school teams — Objective: describe teacher collaboration with caregivers and school staff.
8. Learner-centered teaching — Objective: explain the difference between teacher-led and learner-centered approaches.
9. Teacher wellbeing and boundaries — Objective: describe sustainable teaching habits and appropriate professional boundaries.
10. Becoming an elementary teacher — Objective: summarize what a beginner teacher learns as they begin the role.
