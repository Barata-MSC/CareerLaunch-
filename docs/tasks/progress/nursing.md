### Course 4: Fundamentals of Nursing Care
- Course ID: `nurs-fundamentals-of-care`
- Short code: FNC
- Subtitle: The ideas behind everyday patient care.
- Outcome: Explain the nursing process as a framework and the principles of comfort, safety, and dignity.
- Checklist: [x] `course.js` [x] lessons [x] questions [x] wrapper [x] registered [x] validated
- Lesson outline:
  1. Educational orientation: nursing care foundations — Set the tone for the course and remind learners it is an introductory overview rather than clinical guidance. Include the note: educational orientation, not medical advice or clinical training.
  2. What the nursing process means — Introduce the framework of assess, diagnose, plan, implement, and evaluate in conceptual terms. Note that nursing schools teach the process in more depth.
  3. Assessing a patient in general terms — Explain that assessment means gathering information about a person’s condition and needs. Keep conceptual: no vital-sign ranges, thresholds, or procedures.
  4. Identifying needs and planning care — Describe the diagnose step as naming a patient's care needs (not a medical diagnosis), and how priorities and needs guide a care plan.
  5. Implementing care with safety and dignity — Explain how nursing actions are shaped by safety, comfort, and respect.
  6. Evaluation and adjusting care — Show why care is reviewed and adjusted as needs change.
  7. Comfort, rest, and recovery support — Discuss how nurses help create a comfortable environment that supports healing.
  8. Safety and risk awareness — Explain how reducing risk and raising concerns help support patient safety.
  9. Hygiene, environment, and basic care considerations — Introduce the role of cleanliness and routine care in patient wellbeing. Keep conceptual: no procedures.
  10. Why the basics matter in nursing — Reinforce that daily care routines are guided by professionalism, observation, and compassion.
