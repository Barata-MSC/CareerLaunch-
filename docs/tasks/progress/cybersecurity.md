## Content and safety record

- Total: 2 tracks, 14 courses, 140 lessons, and 140 quiz questions.
- Each course has `quizSize: 10` and `passingPercent: 70`.
- Activities use only `quickCheck`; no code playgrounds, live targets, attack procedures, exploit code, malware, or bypass instructions are included.
- Unauthorized access is described as harmful and illegal. Lessons use fictional scenarios and repeatedly require explicit authorization for professional work.
- Philippine privacy names appear only in Data Privacy Fundamentals; that course names the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and the National Privacy Commission and directs learners to official sources for current requirements.
- The final course in each track addresses roles and continued learning; certification references remain general.
- Supplied links were empty, so no videos or references were invented.
