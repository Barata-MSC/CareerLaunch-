## What was built

- One track, Nursing Student Path, with eight ordered introductory courses.
- Eighty lessons and eighty linked quiz questions, with a 10-question quiz attempt per course.
- Lessons use conceptual explanations, concrete classroom examples, key points, and quick checks. The content is an educational orientation, not medical advice or clinical training.
- Course folders and routes are registered in the shared Learning Hub architecture. The team approved the content and changed Nursing's status to `available`.

## Course-by-course review record

The team reviewed and approved the eight courses. Reviewer name: [Reviewer name]. Review date: [Review date]. The notes below identify the nursing-specific content areas included in that review and can guide future revisions.

### The Nursing Profession and Ethics

- Verify descriptions of nursing roles across hospitals, clinics, schools, and community settings remain broad and do not imply a legal scope of practice.
- Review the treatment of dignity, privacy, confidentiality, consent, professional boundaries, ethical conduct, and accountability.
- Check that teamwork examples avoid assigning duties to specific professions.

### Human Body Basics

- Verify the introductory descriptions of cells, tissues, organs, circulatory, respiratory, digestive, nervous, musculoskeletal, immune, integumentary, endocrine, urinary, and reproductive systems.
- Confirm that the examples stay educational and do not invite learners to assess an individual or draw a health conclusion.
- Check that system interactions are accurately described at this beginner level.

### Communication with Patients and Families

- Review the examples of active listening, open questions, plain language, empathy, nonverbal communication, family involvement, privacy, and team updates.
- Confirm that the examples respect consent and do not imply that silence is agreement or that body language provides certainty.

### Fundamentals of Nursing Care

- Carefully verify the nursing-process sequence: assess, diagnose, plan, implement, evaluate.
- Confirm that “diagnose” is consistently explained as identifying care needs in this framework, not making a medical diagnosis.
- Confirm the note that nursing schools teach the framework in more depth.
- Review all care, comfort, safety, environmental, and hygiene examples to ensure they remain conceptual and do not become procedural guidance.

### Infection Prevention Basics

- Verify the general descriptions of contact, droplets, shared objects, hand hygiene, protective equipment, cleaning, disinfection, respiratory etiquette, waste systems, and the chain model.
- Confirm that no lesson implies a universal method, a guarantee of zero risk, or instructions for selecting or using protective practices.
- Check that learners are directed to current local guidance for setting-specific practices.

### Health Promotion and Community Health

- Review the general descriptions of wellbeing, health education, community conditions, prevention programs, health literacy, and access barriers.
- Confirm the material does not become personal health advice or imply that one lifestyle or program fits everyone.
- Verify that participation, privacy, and community priorities are represented respectfully.

### Patient Safety, Documentation, and Teamwork

- Check that documentation, handoffs, observation, reporting, role awareness, and learning-culture examples are conceptual and do not prescribe operational procedures.
- Review privacy language and the separation of factual observations from interpretations.
- Confirm the examples do not define legal scope or assign profession-specific duties.

### Path to Practice and Caring for Yourself

- Verify the name-level statement that nurses in the Philippines take a licensure examination administered by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC).
- Check that learners are directed to official PRC and Commission on Higher Education (CHED) sources for current requirements, without adding dates, fees, passing rates, coverage, or unsourced requirements.
- Review general descriptions of education, supervised learning, professional growth, wellbeing, boundaries, and varied nursing roles.
- Confirm the closing lesson presents roles and continued learning without employment, salary, or demand claims.
