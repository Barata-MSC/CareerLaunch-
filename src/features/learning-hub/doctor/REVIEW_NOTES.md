## Pre-Medicine and Medical Path

### `med-profession-ethics` — The Medical Profession and Ethics

- Review the broad descriptions of physician roles and collaboration with other professionals.
- Check consent, confidentiality, doing good, avoiding harm, fairness, boundaries, and accountability as introductory ethical frameworks, not legal or clinical instructions.
- Verify the fictional examples remain non-clinical and do not imply guidance for a real care decision.

### `med-biology-foundations` — Biology Foundations

- Review the plain-language explanations of cells, cell structures, DNA, genes, traits, tissues, organs, energy, and internal balance for scientific accuracy.
- Confirm all examples describe general biology and do not imply that genetic or biological concepts predict an individual's health.

### `med-chemistry-foundations` — Chemistry for Medicine

- Review the introductory descriptions of atoms, elements, molecules, bonds, water, mixtures, solutions, pH, reactions, and energy transfer.
- Confirm the examples are scientifically sound and cannot be read as medication, treatment, or personal health guidance.

### `med-body-systems` — Human Body Systems Overview

- Review the normal structure and broad-function descriptions for the skeletal, muscular, nervous, sensory, circulatory, respiratory, digestive, urinary, endocrine, reproductive, immune, and lymphatic systems.
- Check the wording about reproductive systems for age-appropriate, respectful scope.
- Confirm there are no symptom descriptions, disease explanations, screening or vaccination guidance, or instructions for interpreting an individual's health.

### `med-health-communication` — Communication and Empathy in Medicine

- Review the examples about attentive listening, consent-aware communication, privacy, family/support-person involvement, accessibility, and professional limits.
- Confirm examples remain general and do not imply clinical interviewing or counseling instructions.

### `med-clinical-thinking` — Introduction to Clinical Thinking

- Verify all scenarios remain fictional, non-health, and non-diagnostic.
- Review the wording about observations, assumptions, patterns, uncertainty, evidence, bias, and team reasoning so it does not become a method for making real medical decisions.
- Confirm that the course clearly states that professional medical education and supervision are outside this introduction.

### `med-public-health` — Public Health and Health Systems

- Review the general descriptions of public health, community context, prevention as a concept, service settings, health-system roles, partnerships, and systems thinking.
- Confirm no unsupported claims are made about current Philippine service availability, access, eligibility, policy, or official guidance.
- Check that no statistics, outbreak figures, schedules, screening guidance, or personal health recommendations are introduced.

### `med-path-to-practice` — Path to Practice and Resilience

- Review the broad stages of pre-medical studies, medical school, licensure, and further training for accuracy without adding requirements, durations, costs, or fees.
- Verify that the only exam/licensure reference is the spec's name-level statement in this final course: physicians take a licensure examination administered by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), and medical schools may require an admission test.
- Confirm the direction to official PRC, CHED, and school sources is accurate and does not reproduce current requirements or time-sensitive details.
- Review general learning, support, boundaries, and rest language to ensure it is not clinical or mental-health advice.
- Confirm the closing lesson describes roles and continued learning in broad terms and names no specific specialties or credentials.
