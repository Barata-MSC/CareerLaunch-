# Lawyer career expert review notes

Status: Available in the app; expert review sign-off is still incomplete.

The Lawyer career is available in the Learning Hub. All lessons are introductory career orientation, not legal advice or instruction for a real matter.

## Review across both tracks

- Check factual accuracy and terminology for the Philippine context, especially descriptions of public institutions and legal sources.
- Confirm that examples remain fictional classroom exercises and do not resemble advice for a real person or dispute.
- Confirm the educational-not-legal-advice note is the first lesson of every course.
- Check that the course explanations do not cite a specific statute, court decision, or quotation, and do not state time limits, costs, consequences, pass rates, or qualification rules.
- Confirm the treatment of professional roles does not state what paralegals are legally permitted to do. Where role boundaries are mentioned, verify they remain general and defer to current official rules and the relevant organization.
- Confirm the two tracks remain distinct: Lawyer Path emphasizes research, reasoning, writing, and advocacy; Paralegal and Legal Support emphasizes office coordination, document handling, intake, records, and support workflows.
- The two tracks and their routes are registered and available in the career picker.

## Follow-up changes for this review

- Constitutional Law Concepts, lesson 3: renamed to “National and local government” and revised to describe national and local responsibilities in a unitary state with local autonomy.
- Legal Writing and Analysis, lesson 9: renamed to “Separating facts from assumptions.”
- All Lawyer lessons: replaced the repeated third key point with a lesson-specific takeaway; quiz explanations now explain the correct concept and distinguish it from a different course topic.
- Legal Office Operations, lesson 4: clarified that responsibilities vary by office and official rules.
- Court Processes and Filing Basics, lessons 1, 2, 5, 7, 8, and 9: kept the examples administrative and classroom-based, and added variation/official-source caveats where relevant.
- Civil and Criminal Law Overview, lesson 6: clarified standards of proof at a general concept level and excluded assessment of real claims.
- Procedure, Evidence, and Advocacy Basics, lesson 7: reframed the presentation example as a classroom communication exercise, not real-matter strategy.
- Client Intake and Confidentiality, lessons 1–4 and 6–10: reinforced neutral classroom exercises, role clarity, privacy, and the need to check official sources.

## How to preview the content

The Lawyer career is available from the Learning Hub career picker. Open either Lawyer Path or Paralegal and Legal Support, then select a course to preview its lessons.

## Track: Lawyer Path

### `law-profession-ethics` — The Legal Profession and Ethics

- Review the descriptions of lawyer roles, professional ethics, confidentiality, competence, independence, fairness, communication, boundaries, ethical challenges, and responsible advocacy.
- Check that the classroom examples communicate professional habits without turning them into guidance for a real client relationship.

### `law-legal-system` — The Philippine Legal System

- Verify the simplified relationship among the Constitution, statutes, regulations, and court decisions, including the caution that their effect depends on context.
- Check the broad descriptions of the legislative, executive, and judicial branches, law-making, regulations, courts, rights, duties, institutions, and rule of law.
- Confirm the course does not imply a complete or exhaustive hierarchy for resolving a specific question.

### `law-legal-research` — Legal Research Skills

- Check the distinction between primary materials (including constitutions, statutes, regulations, and court decisions) and secondary commentary.
- Review the guidance on source authority, context, recency, note-taking, paraphrase, and recording a research trail.
- Confirm research activities do not become a method for advising on a real legal question.

### `law-legal-writing` — Legal Writing and Analysis

- Review the issue-rule-application-conclusion structure and ensure it is framed as a classroom reasoning method.
- Confirm examples use only supplied hypothetical facts and do not claim an outcome for any real dispute.
- Check that paraphrase, source support, uncertainty, and conclusions are described accurately.

### `law-constitutional-basics` — Constitutional Law Concepts

- Review the introductory descriptions of constitutional frameworks, separation of powers, shared authority, rights, equality, due process, rule of law, and judicial review.
- Ensure no lesson presents a definitive interpretation of a constitutional provision or recommends action in a real situation.

### `law-civil-criminal-overview` — Civil and Criminal Law Overview

- Verify the high-level contrast between private disputes and matters involving public justice institutions.
- Review the explanations of parties, evidence, standards of proof, remedies, and accountability for overgeneralization.
- Confirm these broad labels are not presented as a diagnosis of any real matter.

### `law-procedure-advocacy` — Procedure, Evidence, and Advocacy Basics

- Review the general process map, the role of evidence, relevance, reliability, oral advocacy, questioning, and responsible persuasion.
- Confirm no content gives litigation tactics, predicts outcomes, or teaches a procedure for a real matter.
- Check that process wording does not imply a universal sequence or timeline.

### `law-path-to-practice` — Path to Practice and Client Communication

- Verify the statement that the Supreme Court administers the bar examination and that lawyers belong to the Integrated Bar of the Philippines (IBP).
- Confirm these are name-level orientation statements only, with no current eligibility or other requirements stated.
- Check the direction to consult official sources for current pathway information.
- Review intake, listening, process explanations, empathy, boundaries, and communication examples for accurate professional framing.

## Track: Paralegal and Legal Support

### `law-legal-office-ops` — Legal Office Operations

- Review the descriptions of office workflows, matter records, team roles, client updates, scheduling, document handling, and quality review.
- Confirm role descriptions do not make legal-permission claims about paralegals or other staff.

### `law-document-drafting` — Legal Documents and Drafting Basics

- Check descriptions of document categories, structure, audience, plain language, completeness, source use, consistency, and formatting.
- Confirm examples teach general readability and checking, not drafting a real legal instrument.

### `law-court-filing` — Court Processes and Filing Basics

- Verify the content stays with general administration, document classification, checklists, recordkeeping, review, and courteous communication.
- Confirm calendars and follow-ups are described as office coordination, not instructions about court rules or procedural timing.
- Check that no step implies a universal filing procedure.

### `law-research-support` — Legal Research Support

- Review task scoping, source organization, purposeful reading, summaries, comparisons, neutral fact notes, research memos, and traceability.
- Confirm the support-work framing avoids legal conclusions or claims about what a paralegal may legally do.

### `law-client-intake-confidentiality` — Client Intake and Confidentiality

- Check the treatment of respectful intake, neutral questions, listening, role clarity, confidentiality, sensitive information, and privacy habits.
- Confirm examples do not solicit or expose real personal information and do not provide legal advice.

### `law-legal-tech-records` — Legal Technology and Records Management

- Review the general descriptions of office tools, file naming, access controls, versioning, search, calendars, communication channels, backups, and record trust.
- Confirm technology practices are described as general workplace concepts and do not imply a specific product or verified security configuration.
