# Teacher Career Expert Review Notes

The project owner confirmed team review and approved Teacher availability on 2026-10-21. This checklist records claims and concepts for ongoing reference; no reviewer names are recorded.

## Elementary Teacher track

### `teach-foundations` — Foundations of Teaching

- Check the descriptions of teacher responsibilities, professional boundaries, classroom observation, and collaboration with families and school staff.
- Confirm the example of general Philippine basic-education grade bands: Kindergarten, Grades 1–6 elementary, Grades 7–10 Junior High School, and Grades 11–12 Senior High School.
- Review the privacy and ethics examples for clarity without implying specific legal duties.

### `teach-child-development` — Child Development and Learning

- Review the descriptions of physical, social-emotional, cognitive, language, and self-regulation development for accuracy and appropriate nuance.
- Check the introductory references to Piaget and Vygotsky. They are presented as influential frameworks, not universal predictions or complete explanations of every child.
- Confirm that play, observation, and developmental-difference examples remain general teaching guidance and do not diagnose learners.

### `teach-curriculum-planning` — Curriculum and Lesson Planning

- Review the distinction between broad goals, lesson outcomes, objectives, activities, and assessment evidence.
- Check the illustrative use of Bloom’s taxonomy as a planning framework rather than a required hierarchy or measure of lesson quality.
- Verify that examples are appropriate for elementary learners and do not imply a specific current curriculum mandate.

### `teach-classroom-management` — Classroom Management

- Review the routines, positive behavior support, transitions, conflict response, and classroom-community examples for age appropriateness.
- Confirm that references to a learner being upset or disengaged remain within a teacher’s classroom-support role and do not become counseling or psychological advice.

### `teach-literacy-numeracy` — Teaching Literacy and Numeracy

- Review the introductory explanations of phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, writing development, number sense, one-to-one counting, and early problem solving.
- Check that the suggested classroom examples are accurate and do not overstate a single instructional method.
- Confirm the course does not assign fixed learner profiles based on preferred ways of learning.

### `teach-assessment` — Assessment of Learning

- Review the formative and summative assessment distinction, use of evidence, fairness, rubrics, and feedback examples.
- Confirm that the assessment examples measure their stated learning objectives and avoid claims about official grading requirements.

### `teach-inclusive-education` — Inclusive and Differentiated Instruction

- Review the introductory description of Universal Design for Learning and its flexible engagement, information-access, and response options.
- Check that suggestions for multilingual and additional-needs support remain general, do not prescribe an individual accommodation, and direct readers to current school guidance and appropriate staff.
- Verify the distinction between adapting access and changing a learning goal.

### `teach-professional-growth` — Professional Growth and Licensure Preparation

- Verify the general descriptions of teacher preparation, professional standards, mentoring, portfolios, and education-related roles.
- Confirm the name-level statement that the Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET) is administered by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC).
- Confirm the accompanying note directs learners to official PRC, DepEd, and CHED sources for current requirements. The lesson intentionally stays at a name-level overview and gives no current application details.
- Review portfolio privacy reminders and the note that completing this introductory content does not guarantee a career outcome.

## Secondary Teacher track

### `teach-sec-adolescent-development` — Adolescent Development and Learning

- Review the descriptions of adolescent development, peer influence, identity, motivation, and decision-making for nuance and age appropriateness.
- Check the use of Piaget, Vygotsky, and Bloom’s taxonomy as frameworks rather than fixed predictions or a required sequence.
- Confirm that Grades 7–12 examples reflect varied secondary classroom contexts and do not generalize about all adolescents.

### `teach-sec-content-pedagogy` — Subject Content and Pedagogy

- Review the claims about subject knowledge, modeling, worked examples, active learning, questioning, and addressing misconceptions.
- Check that Bloom’s taxonomy is described as a planning framework and that analogies are identified as limited comparisons.
- Confirm the examples from secondary mathematics, science, English, Filipino, history, and other subjects are age-appropriate.

### `teach-sec-curriculum-design` — Curriculum Design and Unit Planning

- Confirm the general structure of basic education: Kindergarten, Grades 1 to 6 elementary, Grades 7 to 10 Junior High School, and Grades 11 to 12 Senior High School.
- Review the explanations of unit goals, lesson progression, alignment, flexible supports, pacing, and reflective revision.
- Check that planning examples remain illustrative and do not imply a specific current curriculum requirement.

### `teach-sec-classroom-culture` — Classroom Culture and Behavior

- Review the approaches to classroom norms, trust, student agency, consistency, collaboration, and restorative conversations.
- Confirm conflict examples remain educational and avoid assigning fixed character judgments or prescribing counseling.
- Check that the classroom routines are practical for junior and senior high school settings.

### `teach-sec-assessment` — Assessment and Feedback

- Review the distinctions among formative checks, performance tasks, rubrics, self-assessment, peer feedback, and the assessment cycle.
- Check that all feedback examples are actionable, respectful, and aligned to the stated learning objectives.
- Confirm assessment examples do not imply official grading rules.

### `teach-sec-guidance-wellbeing` — Learner Guidance and Wellbeing

- Confirm the course stays at teacher noticing, classroom support, and referral; it must not become diagnosis, medical guidance, psychological advice, or counseling.
- Check that referral language consistently directs learners to follow the school referral process and involve the school counselor or designated staff.
- Review the examples for privacy, professional boundaries, and age-appropriate secondary contexts.

### `teach-sec-career-licensure` — Teaching Career and Licensure Preparation

- Confirm the Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET) is mentioned only by name as administered by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC).
- Verify that the lesson directs learners to official PRC, DepEd, and CHED sources for current requirements without adding time-sensitive pathway details.
- Review the broad descriptions of teacher education, supervised practice, portfolios, professional learning, and possible career directions.
- Check that no course wording promises a particular role or career outcome.
